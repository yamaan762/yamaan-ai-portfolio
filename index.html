Process

From brief to final delivery

  1. STEP 01

    Discovery

    Understand your idea, audience, objective, preferred style and delivery needs.

  2. STEP 02

    Concept Development

    Plan the transformation, visual direction, reference images and scene sequence.

  3. STEP 03

    AI Video Generation

    Create and refine visual stages with suitable AI tools, keeping continuity where possible.

  4. STEP 04

    Editing and Refinement

    Arrange footage, refine pacing and add the transitions and elements agreed in scope.

  5. STEP 05

    Final Delivery

    Deliver the agreed files in the specified format and aspect ratio.
